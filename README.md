# Deep Questions to Ask Your Partner

A curated list of **25 deep questions** for couples who want conversations that go beyond "how was your day?" Use them on a quiet night in, a long walk, or whenever you want to feel closer.

Looking for more? Try **[Question Cards](https://questioncards.app)** — a free couples conversation-card app for iPhone and iPad ([App Store: Question Cards: Couples Game](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6757502263)).

## 25 deep questions

1. What is something you've never told me that you wish I knew?
2. When do you feel most loved by me?
3. What childhood memory still shapes how you see the world?
4. What would you change about our daily routine if you could?
5. What are you most proud of that you rarely talk about?
6. How do you want to grow as a person over the next five years?
7. What fear do you still carry that I might not fully understand?
8. What does "home" mean to you right now?
9. When was the last time you felt truly understood?
10. What do you need more of from me that you haven't asked for?
11. Which friendship of yours means the most, and why?
12. What belief of yours has changed the most since we met?
13. How do you want us to handle conflict when we disagree hard?
14. What adventure would make you feel alive if we did it together?
15. Who in your family do you feel closest to, and what makes that bond special?
16. What does a perfect ordinary Sunday look like for you?
17. Is there a dream you've quietly put on hold?
18. What compliments feel most genuine to you?
19. How do you recharge when life gets overwhelming?
20. What would you want our kids (or future selves) to remember about us?
21. What topic do you wish we talked about more often?
22. When do you feel most secure in our relationship?
23. What song, place, or smell instantly brings you back to a happy memory?
24. How can I better support you when you're stressed?
25. Looking ahead one year, what do you hope we've become better at together?
